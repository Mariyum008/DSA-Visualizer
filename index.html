How it works and helpers you can use
  • Write a function called solve. It receives your input as a: a list of numbers, or a list of characters if you type text.
  • Every time your code reads or changes a, that becomes a step. Swaps are spotted automatically.
  • point('i', i) shows a pointer label under spot i.
  • done(i) paints spot i green.
  • track('Stack', []) or track('Map', {}) makes a stack, queue or hash map that shows up as its own panel.
  • show('sum', sum) shows a variable's value. note('text') adds your own explanation step.
  • Ctrl + Enter runs the code. It runs in a safe sandbox and stops after 4 seconds or 6,000 actions.
